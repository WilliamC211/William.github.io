About

I am an M.A. candidate in Language Intelligence at Sichuan International Studies University, working with Prof. Yu (Adam) Deng. My research sits at the intersection of cognitive linguistics, psycholinguistics, and computational linguistics. I study how grammatical constructions are represented, processed, and learned — combining behavioral experiments (structural priming, maze tasks, eye-tracking), quantitative corpus analysis, and computational modeling (distributional semantics, BERT).
